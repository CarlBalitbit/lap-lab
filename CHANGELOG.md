# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - First development release

- Solo and up to six-car comparisons with preset, custom and seeded random cars.
- Forty circuit profiles with automatic alphabetical circuit discovery.
- Flying qualifying laps and continuous races with standing or rolling starts.
- Configurable races of one to twenty laps, independent lap progression, shared race clock and live standings.
- Offline HTML dashboards, circuit maps, playback-speed slider, chart zoom and position lock.
- Distinct sector/car colors, visible-car follow controls, and smaller anchored corner labels with a visibility toggle.
- CSV telemetry, race results, lap times and JSON session settings.
- Circuit and race validators covering all supplied circuits.

This is initial-development software. Track layouts, corner labels, sectors and elevation profiles are approximate; lap predictions have not been validated against real F1 cars. Most imported circuits use constant reference altitude. Race cars run independently without traffic, tire wear, fuel use or pit stops.
